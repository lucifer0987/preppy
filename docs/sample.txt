Q1. A train 150 m long crosses a pole in 15 seconds. Find its speed.
A) 30 km/h
B) 36 km/h
C) 40 km/h
D) 45 km/h

SOL: Speed = 150/15 = 10 m/s = 10 x 18/5 = 36 km/h.

The rapid expansion of digital payments in India over the past decade has altered the relationship between citizens and formal finance. Where once a bank account was the preserve of the salaried urban household, the combination of low-cost identity verification, a ubiquitous mobile network and a public payments rail has pushed basic financial services into districts that commercial banks had long written off as unviable.

Yet the achievement is more uneven than the headline numbers suggest. Opening an account is not the same as using one, and a substantial share of newly opened accounts record almost no activity beyond the receipt of government transfers.
